The register offsets and bit fields in nvidia/registers/mmio.yaml are taken from
the published register headers of NVIDIA's open GPU kernel modules, under
src/common/inc/swref/published/ (github.com/NVIDIA/open-gpu-kernel-modules):

  nv_ref.h
  turing/tu102/   dev_pmc.h dev_bus.h dev_timer.h dev_fb.h dev_fbpa.h dev_ltc.h
  ampere/ga100/   dev_pmc.h dev_bus.h dev_fb.h
  ampere/ga102/   dev_pmc.h
  ada/ad102/      dev_bus.h
  hopper/gh100/   dev_fb.h dev_fbpa.h dev_therm.h dev_bus.h
  blackwell/gb100/ dev_bus.h dev_pmc_zb.h dev_pmc_zb_addendum.h dev_therm.h
  blackwell/gb202/ dev_therm.h

What was taken is the register map: names, byte offsets and bit fields. No code
from those modules is in this project, and nothing here was obtained by
disassembling or decompiling NVIDIA software. Values are this project's own,
from measurement (registers/measurements/) or from the device profile; each
register's `status` and `measured` keys say which.
