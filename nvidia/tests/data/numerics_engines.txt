# The lines of tools/numerics/mathfn on which the PTX interpreter may differ from the SASS executor (run_numerics_mathfn.sh).
# <program> <function> <precision> <class>, then why. Every other line is the same from both engines.
# All three are NaN payloads, no number differs (docs/numerics/math-functions.md):
mathfn erf f64 edges        erf(double) of a NaN: SASS returns 0x7fffffffe0000000 (the canonical f32 NaN widened), PTX the argument quieted. Not traced.
mathfn erf f64 rand_bits    the same, the NaN arguments among the random bit patterns
mathfn hypot f64 edges      hypot(double) with a signaling NaN and a zero: SASS quiets it, PTX returns it as it came (9 pairs)
mathfn_fast erf f64 edges   as mathfn's
mathfn_fast erf f64 rand_bits   as mathfn's
mathfn_fast hypot f64 edges as mathfn's
