# Licensing

PantheonSim's source is public. From version 0.3.0 it is licensed under the Business Source License 1.1 (BSL), with one
restriction: you may not offer it to other people as a hosted or managed service. Everything else is free. Each version
becomes Apache-2.0, a fully open source license, four years after it is released.

## Free, with no need to ask

- Running it on your laptop, your servers or your cloud account, for your own work.
- Using it in your own CI, including GitHub Actions, GitLab CI and others, in a company or a personal project.
- Open source projects testing their GPU code with it.
- A company running a shared instance for its own staff.
- A consultant running it on a client's machines, for that client.
- Teaching and research at a school, a university or a non-profit, including a class server for students.
- Changing it and redistributing your changes, under this same license.

## Needs a commercial license

Offering PantheonSim to other people as a service. For example:

- a cloud or GPU provider that sells "simulated GPU" machines, or adds them to a product;
- a CI platform that pre-installs it and sells GPU-testing runners;
- an API or website that gives paying users access to simulated GPUs.

Contact saqibkh@pantheonsim.com.

## What stays the same

- Versions up to and including 0.2.0 stay Apache-2.0 (`LICENSE-APACHE-2.0`). Nobody can take that back, and we are not
  trying to.
- Every later version turns into Apache-2.0 on its own date, four years after it is published. The `Change Date` in
  `LICENSE` is moved forward at each release.
- Third-party files in the repository keep their own licenses (see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).
- The name and logo are not licensed. A fork or a hosted service must not present itself as PantheonSim.

## One thing to say plainly

A license that restricts hosting is source-available. It is not open source by the Open Source Initiative's definition,
so from 0.3.0 we describe it as source-available: free except as a hosted service, Apache-2.0 after four years.
